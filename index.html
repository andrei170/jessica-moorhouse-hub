01

1:1 Coaching Is the Only Revenue Layer - Capped at Your Hours

You have 22K followers, a bestselling book, a multi-year podcast, and a fresh QAFP credential from November 2025. The only paid product currently capturing any of that is 1:1 coaching - which runs out of capacity the moment your calendar is full. Every podcast listener and book reader who wants to go deeper with you has nowhere to spend $497-$997 right now. The mid-tier between "I love your content" and "book a session" does not exist.

Cost of Waiting

~$12-29K/mo in programme revenue going to no one. That compounds to ~$150-350K/yr in unbooked revenue while the book and podcast audience grows. Every month the QAFP credential sits unused as a product authority hook is a month of launch momentum lost. That window is open now and narrows as the credential ages in news value.

6 months from now ·~$75-175K never collected. Plus the next wave of podcast listeners who found you in 2026 - same warm audience, same nowhere to go, same competitor building the programme you didn't.

Fix · A $497-$997 "Everything but Money Signature Programme." QAFP-informed, self-paced, monthly live Q&A. Year-1 floor $150-350K direct + incremental 1:1 fill from programme graduates.
Fast Install
02

Book and Podcast Audiences Route to Retail and Spotify - Neither Captures an Email

"Everything but Money" sends buyers to Amazon. More Money Podcast sends listeners to Spotify and Apple. Neither one captures an email, builds a list, or routes toward anything you can sell. The audience is warm and trusting and entirely invisible to your business. A "Canadian Money Clarity Quiz" - 6 questions, life-stage result, custom PDF action plan - converts the passive follower into a tracked email subscriber with a specific money goal you can market to.

Cost of Waiting

~$2-7K/mo in nurture-driven programme and 1:1 sales not happening - ~$30-80K/yr compounding for the life of the list you don't have. 10-15% quiz completion is normal for personal finance audiences with the right hook. Without segmentation, there is no way to separate "debt-payoff stage" from "investment-ready" from "just a fan" - and you can't launch to a list you haven't built.

6 months from now ·Another six months of book sales and podcast episodes with zero email capture. Every listener and reader who left without giving their email is gone from your business permanently.

Fix · "Canadian Money Clarity Quiz" on ScoreApp or Typeform. 6 questions, life-stage result, 3-minute completion. Builds a segmented email list from the audience you already have.
Compound Pickup
03

Podcast Listeners Have No Paid Upgrade - Your Most Loyal Audience Pays Nothing

More Money Podcast listeners spend hours with your voice. They trust you. They've followed you across seasons. Right now there is no way to deepen that relationship with money. No premium feed, no private Q&A, no community. The most loyal segment of your audience - people who have invested real time in you - have nothing to buy that sits between free podcast and 1:1 coaching. That gap is a revenue layer that compounds for the life of the podcast.

Cost of Waiting

~$2-7K/mo in recurring membership revenue that does not exist - the $30-80K/yr ARR layer keeps not getting built each season. Every episode you publish without a membership CTA is another week of compound listener trust that converts to zero revenue.

6 months from now ·Another 24+ episodes published. Same listeners. Same trust. No paid upgrade. The membership flywheel that should already be running starts six months later and earns six months less.

Fix · "More Money Insider" $197-$297/yr annual membership. Monthly Q&A, premium feed, private community. Year-1 floor $30-80K ARR. Compounds with every new episode and season.
