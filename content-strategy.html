Phase 3

Content Strategy - Jessica Moorhouse

Phase 3 deliverable · Hook bank, podcast pivot brief, short-form roadmap, content calendar framework.

Premise

Jessica's 22K @jessicaimoorhouse audience is engaged but under-directed. The current content mix covers personal finance education, book promotion, and podcast episode announcements. None of it routes a follower to a clear next step that captures email or converts toward a paid product. Phase 3 builds a content layer with one job: get the right follower onto the Canadian Money Clarity Quiz.

Podcast Pivot Brief

Current state: The More Money Podcast runs as a standalone content vehicle. Episodes are published, listeners engage, and the relationship ends at the episode boundary. No owned short-form content systematically cut from episodes.

Phase 3 move: Build a short-form content engine from the podcast back-catalog. Each episode becomes 3-5 IG Reels or TikTok cuts. The 40-hook bank above maps to recurring episode themes. One produced episode per week feeds 5 short-form posts. Distribution: IG Reels (primary) + TikTok (secondary).

Why: zero new recording pressure on Jessica, leverages existing IP, gives the back-catalog a second life on short-form, routes viewers to the quiz.

Short-Form Roadmap (90 days)

  • Weeks 1-3: post 4-5 hooks/week from Categories 1-2 (money mindset, debt) - highest-emotional-pain triggers
  • Weeks 4-6: post 4-5/week from Categories 3-4 (income, savings/investing) - highest-practical-relevance triggers
  • Weeks 7-9: post 4-5/week from Categories 5-6 (life stage, book bridge) - community-building and conversion-to-programme triggers
  • Weeks 10-12: post 4-5/week from Categories 7-8 (QAFP credential, podcast) + quiz link in every post bio

Cadence: 4-5 IG Reels + 1 long-form (full podcast episode) per week. Repurpose ratio 1:5 - one recorded conversation = five short-form cuts.

Conversion Goal

Every piece of content has the same final CTA: "Canadian Money Clarity Quiz - 3 minutes, custom action plan, in your inbox" with the link in bio routing through a Linktree hub to the quiz as the primary conversion event.
