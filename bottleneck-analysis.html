Three Bottlenecks I Found Inside Your Funnel - Jessica Moorhouse

All three install in less than 7 days from kickoff. Built to compound. Order by impact.

Bottleneck #1 - 1:1 coaching is the only revenue layer, capped by the hour

The asymmetry: Jessica has 22K followers, a bestselling book, a long-running podcast, and a brand-new QAFP credential. The audience is warm and trust is built. But the only way to work with her costs 1:1 time she does not have an infinite supply of. There is no $497-$997 middle layer where a reader or listener can invest in her expertise without booking her calendar.

A "Everything but Money Signature Programme" - a self-paced online course translating the book's core frameworks into 6 modules with worksheets, QAFP-informed processes, and a monthly group Q&A - captures the $497-$997 buyer who is not ready for 1:1 but is ready to pay for her thinking at scale. The QAFP designation (Nov 2025) is the credibility differentiator: no other Canadian millennial money creator has it. The launch window is open right now.

Why it's costing her: - Every 1:1 coaching hour is a ceiling, not a floor. Revenue is capped at time available. - Podcast listeners and book readers have nowhere to spend $500-$1,000 with her today - The QAFP credential (Nov 2025) is fresh authority that isn't attached to any paid product yet. It decays in news value every month it goes unused. - Conservative Year-1 floor: $150-350K at 300-700 buyers x $497-$997

The fix: - "Everything but Money Signature Programme" - 6-module self-paced course - Modules: money mindset reframe, income clarity, spending systems, debt sequencing, savings architecture (TFSA/RRSP/FHSA), financial identity for the long run - Monthly group Q&A with Jessica (60 min, live + recorded) - $497 standard, $997 with a 30-min personal money review call added - Positioned explicitly as the "install version of the book" - uses her existing IP, no new intellectual lift

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $150-350K/yr at 300-700 buyers.

Why this is #1: Largest ceiling. Largest warm audience to convert (book + podcast listeners). QAFP credential is the launch hook that no competitor can copy. Compounds into 1:1 fill - programme graduates become pre-qualified 1:1 clients.

Bottleneck #2 - Book and podcast audience have no email capture, no segmentation

The leak: "Everything but Money" routes buyers to retail checkout. The More Money Podcast sends listeners to Spotify and Apple. Neither has a mechanism to capture email, segment by life stage, or route toward any next step. The audience is warm and trusting but invisible to Jessica's business infrastructure.

A "Canadian Money Clarity Quiz" - 6 questions, returns a score and a life-stage-specific action plan, captures email and segments by biggest money block (debt, income, savings, investing, mindset) - converts the casual follower into a tracked email subscriber Jessica can market to for the life of the list.

Why it's costing her: - 10-15% quiz completion is normal for personal finance audiences with the right hook = 2-3K incremental email subscribers from existing IG audience alone - Without segmentation, there is no way to isolate "debt-payoff stage" (programme pool) from "investment-ready" (1:1 pool) from "just curious" (podcast + content only) - No email list means no pre-sell of any programme launch, no nurture sequence for book readers, no warm pipeline for 1:1 coaching slots

The fix: - "Canadian Money Clarity Quiz" - 6-question quiz on ScoreApp or Typeform - Captures email, returns a custom 2-page PDF action plan based on life stage - Segments by: debt level, income growth goals, savings stage, biggest money block, coaching readiness - Triggers 8-touch nurture sequence routed by segment (programme pool vs 1:1-ready)

Estimated impact: Year-1 lift $30-80K/yr incremental from email-driven programme sales plus 1:1 fill. Compounds for life of the list.

Why this is #2: Foundational for Bottleneck #1 and #3. Fast install (less than 7 days). The email list is the asset every other bottleneck rides on. Without it, programme launches and 1:1 filling depend entirely on organic post reach.

Bottleneck #3 - Podcast listeners have no paid upgrade path

The contradiction: The More Money Podcast has a loyal multi-year audience. Episodes go out, listeners trust Jessica's voice and perspective, and then the relationship ends. There is no offer to go deeper with her. No paid community, no premium episode tier, no membership with exclusive content.

A "More Money Insider" annual membership ($197-$297/yr) with monthly private Q&A, an ad-free premium podcast feed, a private community (Skool or Circle), and early access to the programme and any future resources turns the passive listener into a recurring revenue asset.

Why it's costing her: - Even 1-2% of a loyal podcast audience converting to $197/yr creates a compounding ARR base - Podcast listeners are the highest-trust segment in the funnel - they have spent hours with Jessica's voice. They are the most likely to pay for ongoing access. - The current model asks nothing of them. No ask = no revenue.

The fix: - "More Money Insider" - $197-$297/yr annual membership - Monthly live Q&A with Jessica (60 min, recorded) - Ad-free premium podcast feed (private RSS) - Private community (Skool or Circle, low-overhead moderation) - Early access to programme content and new resources - Auto-promoted in every podcast episode as a mid-roll CTA

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $30-80K/yr ARR (modest conversion of existing listener base). Compounds with each new episode and season.

Why this is #3: Lower ceiling than #1, dependent on Bottleneck #2 for email nurture infrastructure. But it locks in listener LTV which compounds podcast economics for the life of the show.
