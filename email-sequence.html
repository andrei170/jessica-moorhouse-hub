Phase 1+2

Email Sequences - Jessica Moorhouse

Phase 1 + 2 deliverable · Pre-call nurture (3 touches), post-call (4 touches over 14 days), 10-day welcome flow.

Sequence 1 · Welcome Flow (10 days, 8 touches)

Triggered when a new subscriber completes the Canadian Money Clarity Quiz. Segmented by life stage (early-stage / mid-stage / 1:1-ready).

T+0 - Immediate (Quiz Result)

Subject: Your Canadian Money Clarity result + what to do this week Body: Quiz result reveal, 1-paragraph context on what the score means for their specific life stage, 3-bullet action plan, link to download the custom PDF action plan, soft CTA toward the book "Everything but Money." Goal: deliver the promise, set expectations, build immediate trust.

T+1 day - The Bridge

Subject: The thing most money books never tell you Body: A specific insight from the process of writing "Everything but Money" - the emotional truth behind the practical advice. The moment Jessica realised the problem was never the math. Closes with "The Signature Programme is where I turn this into your actual plan." Goal: bridge quiz taker → programme buyer by making the book's promise feel personally relevant.

T+3 days - The Debt Story

Subject: The debt payoff order that isn't avalanche or snowball Body: A specific, step-by-step framework for Canadian debt sequencing. References HELOC rates, credit card vs student loan dynamics. CTA → Everything but Money Signature Programme landing page. Goal: prove practical depth beyond inspiration. Show the QAFP-level thinking.

T+5 days - The TFSA/RRSP Story

Subject: The savings decision most Canadians get wrong in their 30s Body: The TFSA vs RRSP decision explained through a real client example (anonymised). Not generic. Canadian-specific. CTA → programme landing. Goal: build trust through specificity. Canadian readers recognise real Canadian advice.

T+7 days - The Everything but Money Bridge

Subject: The chapter I almost didn't write Body: The personal money story behind one section of the book. Why it was hard to put in. Why it's the one chapter readers tell her changed something. The Signature Programme is the install version. CTA → programme. Goal: route book readers into the buying frame through a personal disclosure.

T+9 days - Soft Ask

Subject: Quick check before I move on Body: Two-line check-in. "Did any of this land? Are you at the point where a system would actually help?" Link to programme landing. "Or hit reply and tell me where you're stuck." Goal: segment hot vs warm; route hot to the landing page, warm to a 1:1 reply for triage.

T+10 days - Last Touch (segmented)

  • Score High (1:1-ready): "You don't need the programme. You need a session." → routes to 1:1 coaching booking page.
  • Score Mid (Programme pool): Last call for the $497 standard tier. CTA → programme landing.
  • Score Low (Early-stage): "Start with the book. Then we talk." → Amazon link + book QR for quiz re-take in 6 months.

Sequence 2 · Pre-Call Nurture (3 touches before 1:1 coaching call)

Triggered when someone books a 1:1 coaching discovery call or the optional 1:1 review add-on from the programme.

T-7 days

Subject: Before our call - three things that will make it more useful Body: Three prep questions: current monthly income and spending breakdown (approximate), biggest one money concern right now, one specific goal for the next 12 months. Framed warmly, not as homework.

T-3 days

Subject: What I've seen in 10 years of money coaching Body: Story-driven. One specific pattern Jessica has seen repeatedly in her coaching that rarely shows up in books or podcasts. Sets the frame for a useful conversation.

T-1 day

Subject: Tomorrow at [time] - one thing to bring Body: Calendar reminder, one specific prep ask (a screenshot of their credit card balance or TFSA contribution room), and a note that the call is theirs to use however is most useful.

Sequence 3 · Post-Call Follow-Up (4 touches over 14 days)

Triggered after any 1:1 coaching call regardless of outcome.

T+1 hour - Thank You + Recap

Subject: Recap from our call + 2 next steps Body: 1-paragraph recap of what was discussed, 2 specific action items agreed on the call, link to the agreed-upon path (programme / follow-up call / wait list).

T+3 days - The Specific Resource

Subject: The [resource] I mentioned on the call Body: Whatever specific resource Jessica mentioned (a TFSA contribution calculator, a debt sequencing worksheet, a recommended book chapter). Sent as a clean PDF or direct link.

T+7 days - The Check-In

Subject: Did you try [the thing]? Body: Soft accountability nudge. "Reply with one line if you did. Reply with a question if you didn't." No pressure, just presence.

T+14 days - The Decision

Subject: Where you landed Body: Clean ask: "Are we moving forward with [agreed path] or holding off?" No pressure. Clear decision frame. Either route triggers the right next sequence (programme onboarding or long-term nurture).

Subject Line Bank (rotation)

For ongoing broadcasts beyond the welcome sequence:

  • "The number Canadians never track (and it matters most)"
  • "What I'd do if I were starting from $0 in Canada in 2026"
  • "The TFSA mistake I made at 28"
  • "Salary negotiation in Canada - what actually works"
  • "A listener question I keep getting"
  • "What the QAFP changed about how I give advice"
  • "The most honest thing I can say about building wealth"

Triggers + Tags

  • Tag by quiz life-stage tier on completion
  • Tag by purchase (Programme $497 / $997 / 1:1 coaching booking)
  • Tag by content engagement (opened 5+ in 30 days = "warm subscriber")
  • Auto-suppress promotional during any personal announcement or major news cycle in personal finance
  • Re-engagement campaign at 60 days of inactivity ("Still there? One question.")
