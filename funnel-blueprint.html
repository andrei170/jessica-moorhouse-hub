Tech Stack (recommended, swappable)

  • CMS / Course host: Kajabi (preferred - all-in-one) or Teachable
  • Landing pages: Webflow (control) or Kajabi (speed)
  • Email: Kit / ConvertKit (preferred for creator audiences) or Kajabi-native
  • Quiz / Scorecard: ScoreApp (preferred for segmentation) or Typeform
  • Payment: Stripe (programme) + Whop or Gumroad (membership if More Money Insider expands)
  • Video host: Wistia (programme) + YouTube (long-form) + Loom (1:1 personal review calls)
  • Analytics: GA4 + Meta Pixel + Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps)
  • Community / Membership (More Money Insider): Skool (preferred - low overhead) or Circle
  • Scheduling: Calendly (1:1 booking)
  • Project / build: Notion (handover-friendly)

Integration Points

  1. Quiz → Email: ScoreApp triggers Kit on quiz completion with life-stage segment tag
  2. Email → Programme: Kit triggers Kajabi enrolment on purchase webhook
  3. Programme → 1:1 Coaching: Programme graduates routed to 1:1 application form on completion of module 6
  4. Book QR → Quiz: print QR routes directly to quiz entry page
  5. All events → GA4 + Meta Pixel via GTM: captures full funnel attribution
  6. Podcast mid-roll → More Money Insider: direct link in episode show notes and mid-roll CTA

Owner Map

  • Jessica: filming (VSL preview + any photoshoot), monthly live Q&A on programme, podcast recording, top-line decisions on copy + brand, regulatory review of any financial content
  • AG1 (Andrei): everything else - landing page build, email writing, quiz setup, automation wiring, content production cycles, ads management, weekly reporting

Failure Modes + Mitigations

  • Regulatory delay on programme content (QAFP context): position all content as financial education, not regulated advice. Add standard Canadian disclaimer. No licensed advice required for an educational course structure.
  • VSL filming delay: use existing podcast audio or a recorded webinar replay as the Phase 1 placeholder; replace with custom VSL after launch.
  • Book QR delay (publisher cycle): run digital QR campaign first via IG bio + podcast show notes; physical update follows next print run or next order of author copies.
  • Quiz over-segmentation: start with 3 tiers (early-stage / mid-stage / 1:1-ready); expand to 5 only after 500+ quiz completions confirms volume by tier.

KPIs (90-day)

  • Phase 1: Everything but Money Signature Programme landing live · 30+ buyers in first 30 days
  • Phase 2: 500+ quiz completions · 8-12% IG-to-email conversion
  • Phase 3: 4-5 IG Reels per week shipped · podcast back-catalog repurposed into 20+ short clips
  • Phase 4: Meta CPL under $15 (personal finance lead gen) · ROAS 3x by week 12
  • Year-1 floor target: $210-510K incremental on top of existing 1:1 coaching and book revenue
