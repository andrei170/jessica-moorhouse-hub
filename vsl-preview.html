VSL Preview Script - Jessica Moorhouse

Phase 1 deliverable · Short preview script structure, page layout notes, production notes for the Everything but Money Signature Programme hero asset.

Format

Short VSL preview: 4-6 minutes, not a full 18-minute presentation. This is the landing-page hero that converts a warm quiz completer or podcast listener into a programme enrolment. Single talking-head setup, warm and approachable. No B-roll required for the preview version.

Cold Open (00:00 - 00:30)

On camera, Jessica:

"I wrote a book about money. I did not write it to help people budget better. I wrote it because I kept seeing the same thing - people who earned good money, did all the right things on paper, and still felt like they were behind. And it was never actually about the money. It was about everything they had never been taught. That's the book. This video is about what comes next."

The Stakes (00:30 - 01:30)

What the average Canadian millennial or Gen Z reader is dealing with right now:

  • Earns a solid income and still feels financially anxious
  • Has a TFSA but isn't sure if they're using it right
  • Carries credit card debt from a year or two ago and is not gaining ground
  • Has read financial advice online and found it either too American or too generic to actually apply
  • Wants a plan, not just principles - something to follow on a Tuesday

Specific and Canadian: a 30-year-old earning $72K in Toronto who puts $400/month into their TFSA and $200/month toward a Visa balance earning 19.99% APR is losing money every month they don't resequence. The programme finds that and fixes it in module two.

Who I Am (01:30 - 02:30)

Concise credentials, not a career history:

  • Host of the More Money Podcast (multi-year back-catalog)
  • Author of "Everything but Money" (bestselling)
  • QAFP designation, November 2025 (Qualified Associate Financial Planner - FP Canada certified)
  • 10+ years helping Canadians talk about money without shame

The point is not the credentials. The point is that the programme is built on tested frameworks, not content.

What "Everything but Money" Got Right (02:30 - 03:30)

The book gave readers permission to be honest about their money situation without shame. What it did not do - because a book cannot - is build your actual plan. The Signature Programme does that. It takes the core frameworks from the book, turns them into 6 modules with real worksheets and a step-by-step process, and adds a monthly live Q&A with Jessica. The book is the why. The programme is the what and the how.

The 6-Module Framework (03:30 - 04:45)

A brief walkthrough of each module:

  1. Your Money Story - identify the beliefs you inherited about money and which ones are costing you now
  2. Income Clarity - what you actually earn, what you keep, and the gap most Canadians never close
  3. Debt Sequencing - the Canadian-specific payoff order (not the American avalanche/snowball you've read about)
  4. Savings Architecture - TFSA, RRSP, FHSA in the right order for your specific situation
  5. Spending Systems - a plan that accounts for your real life, not a spreadsheet that works for three weeks
  6. The Long Game - financial identity and the habits that compound over 20 years

Each module includes written lessons, worksheets, and a short recorded Q&A segment from Jessica.

Who This Is For (04:45 - 05:15)

This is for: Canadians in their 20s-40s who have the income and the desire to sort out their finances but not a clear plan. Readers of "Everything but Money" who want to apply the thinking. Podcast listeners who have been meaning to act for six months.

This is not for: people who need regulated financial advice specific to their investment portfolio (that is 1:1 coaching). People who have no income yet. People who want a get-rich scheme.

Close (05:15 - end)

The programme link is below the video. Enrolment takes two minutes. The first win usually happens in module two, within the first two weeks.

"If Everything but Money made you feel less alone with your money, the programme is what actually gets you moving. The link's right below. - Jessica."

Production Notes

  • Filming location: Jessica's home office or a clean, warm background (bookshelf preferred)
  • Camera: Standard talking-head setup. iPhone 15 Pro or better is sufficient for the preview version.
  • Wardrobe: Clean, approachable. Not corporate. Not casual. Mid-register.
  • Lighting: Natural light from a window + one soft fill light. Warm, not clinical.
  • Pace: Conversational, not rushed. Slight Canadian cadence is a feature, not a bug.
  • Music: None under the talking head. Optional light ambient under any cut sequences.
  • Thumbnail (Wistia poster): Jessica to camera, warm background. Title overlay: "If you've read the book - this is what you do next."

Short Cuts (Phase 3 content)

From the long-form version (if filmed): - 1 x 90-second "what the QAFP means for this programme" cut for IG/TikTok - 3 x 45-second module-specific cuts (one per module x 3 lead modules) - 1 x 60-second cold open cut for paid traffic
